# Strategy guide — Word Debauchee

Strategy only; the server teaches the rules. Written from the rules and the AI notes
(the integration runbook's `STRATEGY-GUIDE.md`).

## What to value

- **Points come from the synonym's length minus the reveals.** A long synonym cracked
  early is the big payout; a short one after three reveals is a point. As a guesser,
  aim at the longest synonym you are fairly sure of, not the first one you think of.
- **As the debauchee, the word matters more than the clues.** A long word with short
  synonyms pays well if you are stumped and pays little if you are cracked. Keep the
  card whose synonyms are long and rare.

## The debauchee

- Of the four clue cards, bury the gift. Shortest synonym, First letter and Last
  letter give the most away; Letters, Vowels and Consonants the least. But Category
  and Type together narrow the word more than most people expect, so do not play
  both if you can help it.
- Reveal the common letters first (E, A, T, O) and keep the odd ones (Q, Z, J, X)
  for last; a rare letter is a clue, a common one is nearly nothing.
- A guesser who calls "Reveal!" is paying a point for a letter. The more they call, the
  better your round goes even if they crack it.

## The guessers

- Talk. Hint around the synonym you want ("the opposite of sad") and let the others
  build toward it. Never say the word: a said word is burned for the round.
- Build the synonym the clues point at hardest, and check on the tile that completes
  it; a wrong check costs nothing but the turn's tile.
- Call "Reveal!" when the clues still leave too many words open and the letters on
  the table are going nowhere. Each reveal costs every guesser a point of the payout,
  so once a word is nearly spelled, finish it instead.
- Under Bribe, a clue is usually worth more than a letter: take the deck whose kind of
  clue you have heard least.

## The House

- You are all one team, so coordinate out loud: pick one synonym and build it
  together, one tile each per turn.
