# Word Debauchee — rules

**2–8 players · 20–40 minutes · ages 10+**

One player, the **debauchee**, holds a secret word and gives away three stingy clues.
Everyone else races to spell a **synonym** of that word from a pile of letter tiles, talking
all the while, but never allowed to say the word they are spelling or to tell anyone which
letter to grab. Every 30 seconds the debauchee lays down one more letter of the secret word,
and the guessers' payout shrinks. The best debauchee wins.

## Components

| Item | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Letter tiles | 144 | Distribution in the appendix |
| Word cards | 500 | One word each, with its facts and its synonym list |
| Clue cards | 48 | Two decks: 20 **number clues**, 28 **word clues** (list in the appendix) |
| Sand timer | 1 | 30 seconds |
| Score pad | 1 | |

### A word card

```
  HAPPY
  letters 5 · vowels 1 · consonants 4 · syllables 2
  category Feelings · type Adjective
  grade 1 · difficulty ●○○
  ──────────────────────────────────────────
  glad · merry · jolly · joyful · cheerful
  content · pleased · upbeat
```

The top half is the word's **facts**, so the debauchee can answer any clue card without
counting or judging. The bottom half is the **synonym list**, which is the referee: a
spelled word scores if and only if it is on the list. (A, E, I, O and U are vowels; Y is a
consonant. Grade is the US school grade the word is usually taught in.)

### A clue card

Each clue card names one thing the debauchee must tell the table about the secret word.
Number clues have a number for an answer: *Letters*, *Vowels*, *Grade*, *Shortest
synonym*. Word clues don't: *Category*, *First letter*, *Rhymes with*, *Opposite*.

## Setup

1. Tip all 144 tiles face up in the middle of the table and spread them so everyone can
   reach them. This is the **pool**. Leave an open space beside it: the **build area**.
2. Shuffle the word cards into a face-down deck. Shuffle the number clues and the word
   clues into two separate face-down decks.
3. Whoever most recently got away with something is the first debauchee.

## A round

### 1. The debauchee picks a word

The debauchee draws three word cards, secretly chooses one, and slides the other two
under the deck. Keep the chosen card hidden.

### 2. The debauchee gives three clues

The debauchee draws **two number clues and two word clues**, looks at them in secret,
and chooses **three** to play. They lay the three face up where everyone can see them and
answer each one out loud, truthfully. (Four cards and three to play means at least one
number clue and at least one word clue are always among them.) The fourth card goes under
its deck.

Nothing else may be said about the word, now or later, but any guesser may ask the
debauchee to repeat an answer at any time. Then the debauchee flips the timer.

### 3. The guessers build

Play passes clockwise among the guessers, starting with the player to the debauchee's
left, and keeps cycling until the round ends. On your turn do exactly **one** of these:

- **Place** one tile from the pool into the build area.
- **Move** one tile already in the build area, or return it to the pool.
- **Call "Reveal!"** The debauchee immediately reveals a letter (see step 4) and
  restarts the timer. Use this when you'd rather have more information than more time.

You may not pass. Build wherever you like: several candidate words can sit in the build
area at once.

**Talking.** Guessers may talk freely, with two exceptions:

- **Never say the word you want spelled.** Any word a guesser says out loud is *burned*
  and cannot score this round, even if it is on the list. Hint around it: "the opposite
  of sad", "what a dog does with a bone".
- **Never tell or suggest a letter to another player.** No "starts with G", no "you need
  an L", no pointing at tiles.

The debauchee may listen, smirk and say nothing. They never confirm or deny guesses
about the secret word.

**Checking a word.** When your tile completes a word you believe is a synonym, say
"Check!" and point at it. The debauchee looks at the card and answers only **"yes"** or
**"no"**. A word reads only if its tiles sit touching, in one straight line, left to right.
A "no" word may stay on the table; players can keep moving its tiles.

### 4. The timer runs out

When the sand runs out, the current turn finishes, then the debauchee **reveals**: they
take one tile matching a letter of the secret word from the pool and place it face up in
a row in front of them. The debauchee chooses which letter; positions are not shown.
If the pool has no tile of that letter, the debauchee takes one from the build area
instead. Then they flip the timer and play resumes with the next guesser in turn order.

### 5. The round ends

The round ends the instant one of these happens:

- A check gets a **"yes"**. The guessers cracked it.
- The debauchee places the **last letter** of the secret word in front of them. The
  guessers are stumped.

The debauchee turns the card face up and reads the word.

## Scoring

**Stumped:** the debauchee scores the number of letters in the secret word. Guessers
score nothing.

**Cracked:** every guesser scores the number of letters in the synonym **minus** the
number of tiles the debauchee has revealed, with a minimum of **1**. The debauchee
scores nothing.

| Round | Synonym | Revealed | Each guesser | Debauchee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HAPPY, cracked with GLAD | 4 | 0 | 4 | 0 |
| HAPPY, cracked with CHEERFUL | 8 | 2 | 6 | 0 |
| HAPPY, cracked with GLAD | 4 | 4 | 1 | 0 |
| HAPPY, never cracked | — | 5 | 0 | 5 |

Write the scores down, return every tile to the pool, and the player to the debauchee's
left becomes the next debauchee.

## Winning

The game ends once every player has been the debauchee the same number of times:
once each with 5–8 players, twice each with 2–4. Highest total wins; ties share the win.

## Example round (4 players)

Ann is the debauchee. She draws HAPPY, VILLAGE and STROLL and keeps HAPPY. Her clue
cards are *Letters*, *Shortest synonym*, *Type* and *Opposite*. *Shortest synonym* would
tell the table "4", a gift, so she buries it and plays the other three: "Five letters.
It's an adjective. The opposite is SAD." Timer flips.

Ben places a G. Cat says "oh, like the opposite of sad?" and places an L. Dev places an A.
Ben adds a D, says "Check!" and points. Ann checks the card: GLAD is listed. "Yes."

Ann revealed nothing, so Ben, Cat and Dev each score 4. Ann scores 0. Ben is the next
debauchee.

A slower round: after 30 seconds Ann reveals a P and flips the timer. After 60 seconds she
reveals the second P. Dev calls "Reveal!" on his turn and Ann lays down the Y. Cat finally
finishes MERRY. Each guesser scores 5 − 3 = 2.

## Variants

- **Laps instead of sand.** Drop the timer: the debauchee reveals a letter each time the
  turn comes back round to the first guesser. Good for 7–8 players, where a 30-second
  timer gives some players no turn at all.
- **Bribe.** When a guesser calls "Reveal!", the guessers may take a clue instead of a
  letter: the caller draws the top card of either clue deck and the debauchee answers it.
  A bribed clue counts as a revealed tile for scoring.
- **Open hand.** Kinder for new players: skip the draw and let the debauchee choose any
  three clue cards from the whole set, at least one of them a number clue.
- **Two-player duel.** One guesser, no turn order: they place tiles freely while the sand
  runs. Play four rounds each.
- **The House.** For the digital edition: the computer is the debauchee every round and
  everyone at the table guesses, scoring as one team against it. Nobody at the table has
  a secret, so one shared screen is fine. Play as many rounds as a normal game at that
  table size; highest total wins, the table or the house.
