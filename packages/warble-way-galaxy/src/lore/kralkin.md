**Physiology.** Kralkin are bipedal creatures with two upper-body appendages which can be used for flight and two lower-body appendages for ambulation. They require a mixture of CO2 and N for respiration and consume class 1 plant matter. The average Kralkin is 7–8 feet tall with a wingspan of up to 15 feet. Kralkin communicate by pushing CO2 through their esophagus and contracting muscles located inside their beaks.

**Homeworld.** Kralkin originate from the planet **Arraak**, but took to interplanetary travel very early — their home planet is not very densely populated and is seen more as a luxury destination than a place to live. Most Kralkin prefer to live on space platforms scattered around their home solar system; they also maintain three very large space stations near large asteroid fields.

**Society.** Kralkin society is an oligarchy: an Emperor or Empress and a small court decide laws and governance for the homeworld and the space stations (see the faction **The Royal Hegemony**).

**Standing in the Alliance.** The Kralkin discovered and allied with the Hexapods early — the first version of the Galactic Alliance. With the largest space fleet of all members, they transport and protect Hexapods across the galaxy. They trade openly with Humans but resent Human-Hexapod closeness.

**Names.** Common male names: Gronk, Kakaaw, Arrak. Common female names: Pii, Tweet, Chirp.

**In play.** Kralkin idealize **Moxie** (LEA/DIP/ACT) — social grace impresses them when recruiting.
