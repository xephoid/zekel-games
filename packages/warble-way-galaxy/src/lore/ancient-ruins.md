---
title: The Ancient Ruins
summary: Impossibly old outposts that mirror the Alliance races' own architecture, filled with functioning super-technology, lethal security, and infestations.
tags: ruins, mystery, technology, diggers
---

When the Kralkin first started to explore outside their solar system, they discovered the ruins of old outposts and space platforms. The strangest thing about these places was that they appeared to be **built by Kralkin**. Later they found ruins that seemed **Human-built**, and even some advanced-looking **Grull** structures.

Stranger still: when they attempted to carbon-date the ruins, the results showed them to be much older than any known history — **dating back millions of years**.

Despite their age, some ruins contain weapons and other technologies that still function. This equipment is often far more advanced than any known technology developed today. For this reason the ruins are a popular site for diggers, scavengers, and treasure hunters. Since all of the safest ruins have been picked clean, the ones left can be extremely dangerous — some still have lethal security systems, and others have become infested with dangerous creatures.

Nobody knows who built them, why they mirror the Alliance races' own forms, or what waits at the deepest levels. This is the galaxy's central mystery — use it sparingly and never resolve it outright.
