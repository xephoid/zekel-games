---
title: Humans
summary: Aggressive, militaristic refugees of a dead Earth, living aboard four city-sized Megaships. They idealize Brawn.
tags: race, humans, megaships, earth
---

**Physiology.** Humans are bipedal creatures with two upper-body appendages used for grasping and climbing and two lower-body appendages for ambulation. They require O2 for respiration and consume class 3 plant and animal matter for sustenance. An average human is smaller than most other Galactic Alliance members, reaching about 5–6 feet tall with a similar wingspan (humans cannot fly). Humans communicate by pushing O2 through their esophagus while contracting muscles located at the end of their soft mouths.

**Homeworld.** Earth, the human homeworld, has been uninhabitable for over a century — planet-wide pollution drove the survivors off some 120 years ago. All humans live on massive starships known as **Megaships**, of which there are **four**, each housing about a million humans. The Megaships are very slow-moving and generally remain close to Earth's star, known as Sun.

**Society.** Human society is plutocratic. A small set of wealthy individuals make most of the decisions that decide laws and governance on the Megaships. They do have elected officials and a democratic structure, but this is primarily symbolic of human ideals.

**Standing in the Alliance.** Humans have a tenuous relationship with the other members. They are very aggressive and militaristic, yet their numbers and firepower are by far the weakest. The Kralkin happily trade and open their spaceports to Humans, but don't like the Humans working closely with the Hexapods. There is also a cultural stigma: a Kralkin delicacy is the testicles of a creature closely resembling an evolutionary ancestor of the Humans called an Ape.

**Names.** Common male names: Zhang, Michael, Muhammad. Common female names: Wang, Katie, Aaliyah.

**In play.** Humans idealize **Brawn** (SWA/ARM/DEM) — appeal to strength when recruiting them.
