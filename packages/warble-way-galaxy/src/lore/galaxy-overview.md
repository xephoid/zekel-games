title: The Warble Way Galaxy

Only the Kralkin and Humans care about the name of the galaxy. Recently the two races agreed upon the name **The Warble Way Galaxy** — a combination of what the Kralkin and Humans used to call it.

The **Galactic Alliance** recognizes four member races: **Kralkin, Hexapod, Human and Grull**.

The Kralkin will say they have been spacefaring the longest — about the last 250 years — though nobody knows how long the Hexapods have been a multi-planet species. Hexapods grow from airborne spores that can survive the vacuum of space for very long periods, and they don't conceive of time the way Kralkin, Humans, or even Grull do.

The Kralkin quickly discovered and entered into a close alliance with the Hexapods — the first version of the Galactic Alliance. Hexapods don't seem to care much about allegiances, but they are amicable to the arrangement: the Kralkin have the largest space fleet of all the Alliance members and help the Hexapods by transporting and protecting them wherever they go.

The second species to take to the stars were the Humans, fleeing an uninhabitable Earth on massive city-sized Megaships. Their relationship with the rest of the Alliance is tenuous — aggressive and militaristic, yet by far the weakest in numbers and firepower.

The most recent members are the Grull, whose technology might be considered primitive — their spacecraft are basically large rock formations hurled into space — but whose hospitality is second to none in the galaxy.
