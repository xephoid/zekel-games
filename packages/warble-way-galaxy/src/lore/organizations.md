The commercial powers of the Warble Way Galaxy, and what a shop's shelves carry under their marks:

- **Aarak Pharmaceuticals and Subsidiaries** — everything; the conglomerate touches every market in the galaxy.
- **WinchesterZhang** — weapons and armor.
- **TanakaRodriguez** — demolitions.
- **Alhambra** — melee weapons.
- **800800** — mobility and scientific gear.

Use these names to dress shop inventories, mission patrons, cargo manifests, and habitat marketplaces — a "+1 GUN weapon" reads better as WinchesterZhang surplus, and a Personal Teleporter is exactly the kind of thing 800800 would license.
