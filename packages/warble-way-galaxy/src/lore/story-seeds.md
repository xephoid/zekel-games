Designer-written scene material. When a mechanical event fires (a ♠ hazard, a ♦ pirate, a mission), you may dress it with one of these — or improvise in their spirit. The mechanics (which abilities, which minimums, which consequences) always come from the server, never from these sketches.

**Named characters:**
- **Krazz** — a notorious Kralkin outlaw and pirate leader who lurks in treacherous asteroid fields, demanding hefty sums of credits for safe passage.
- **General Barak Kennedy** — leader of the United Human Military.

**Hazard dressings** (the designer's own narrative versions of table hazards):
- You arrive at a refueling station to realize it is being robbed — the robbers are extremely well armed. Sneak up and subdue them, or find someone nearby who can help.
- A strange, extremely resilient mold has started growing in the ship; all traditional attempts to eradicate it have proven fruitless.
- A gunfight breaks out at a sustenance vendor — the other patrons don't seem surprised; this must be a regular occurrence.
- The ship enters a particularly dense asteroid field, freshly made from a collision of two large asteroids.
- A distress beacon leads to a deserted ship; all attempts to hail are met with silence. Whatever killed the crew may still be aboard.
- A scared, starving stowaway is discovered — just before the authorities stop you and demand to search your ship for a fugitive.
- A deal to sell some old sustenance cubes goes wrong when the buyer discovers they are not as fresh as claimed — and pulls a pistol.
- A strange spacecraft hails you; the being on screen is like none you have ever seen, and it is looking for a fight.

**Scenario hooks:**
- **The Planetary Siege** — a small mining colony's distress signal; heavily armed space pirates have seized the mining equipment and trapped the colonists.
- **Diplomatic Standoff at Outpost 7** — at a neutral trading hub on the galaxy's edge, Kralkin and Grull ambassadors accuse each other of stealing resources; it's a matter of time before violence.
- **A Diplomatic Gala Gone Wrong** — aboard an elaborate space station, an argument between races escalates toward an interstellar incident.
- **Space Pirate Standoff** — Krazz demands tribute in an asteroid field; talk, fight, or endure.
