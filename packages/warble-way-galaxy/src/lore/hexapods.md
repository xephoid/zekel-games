title: Hexapods

**Physiology.** Hexapods are six-limbed cephalopods. They require H2O and S for respiration and consume class 3 plant matter. The average hexapod is 6 feet long, but they have no bones, so they can easily squeeze into very tight spaces. They communicate telepathically. Hexapods mostly travel in groups of six inside robotic platforms that allow them to easily traverse land and, in some cases, air.

**Origins.** It is not known when or where Hexapods originated. If asked, a hexapod will respond that they have always been everywhere. There is not enough evidence to confirm or deny this. They grow from airborne spores that can survive the vacuum of space for very long periods, and they don't conceive of time the way the other races do.

**Society.** Hexapod society appears to be anarchy. There are no clear social structures beyond the bond of a group of six creatures — a **pod**. Little is known about individual hexapods; pods usually refer to themselves as a single individual with one name.

**Names.** Hexapods use hexadecimal numbers to identify themselves. Example names: F0C706, 7EE700, 080085.

**In play.** Hexapods idealize **Smarts** (HAK/RES/MEC) — demonstrate knowledge when recruiting them. An ACT-trained performer may roll an extra die when recruiting Hexapods.
