title: Grull
summary: Large, mild-tempered serpentine hive people of Grull Prime whose ships are hurled rocks and whose hospitality is legendary. They idealize Finesse.

---

**Physiology.** The Grull are large serpentine creatures with four upper-body limbs: one larger set for grasping and climbing, one smaller set for digging and slashing. They also have horns, and some have chitinous plating. They require O2 to respirate but can survive long periods without it — including the vacuum of space. They consume class 1 animal matter. Grull vary greatly in size: some fully mature Grull reach only 4 feet tall, while others grow as tall as 15 feet. They communicate through a complex system of body movement, teeth chattering, purring, and claw scraping.

**Homeworld.** The Grull originate from a planet they call **Grull Prime**. Having only recently discovered space travel, they are the newest members of the Galactic Alliance. Grull spacecraft are basically large rock formations that have been hurled into space.

**Society.** Grull society is hive-like. Multiple queens produce hundreds of offspring with a handful of males. Only about 5% of Grull are male, and queens are very protective of their male partners — very few non-Grull have ever seen a male Grull. Female Grull are bred for specific roles: workers, soldiers, diplomats, scientists. Relations between hives are mostly peaceful. Any female Grull may become a queen by joining with a male, but most don't want to — queens have extremely limited mobility. (See **Factions** for the four noteworthy queens.)

**Temperament.** While Grull appearances seem aggressive, they are mainly mild-tempered. Since they appear frightening to both Kralkin and Humans, they go to great lengths to accommodate the other Alliance members — Grull hospitality is second to none in the galaxy.

**Names.** Most Grull names are unpronounceable to races who communicate audibly; notable Grull choose stone-names for outsiders' benefit.

**In play.** Grull idealize **Finesse** (PIL/GUN/SNE) — grace and precision win their respect when recruiting.
