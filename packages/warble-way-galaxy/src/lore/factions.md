**The Royal Hegemony «Eagle sound».** The Kralkin Emperor «Eagle sound» and his court — the oligarchy that decides laws and governance for Arraak and the Kralkin space stations.

**FFFFFF.** Anarchist scientists who are constantly trying to make sure all scientific discoveries are free to all.

**UHM — United Human Military.** Leader: General Barak Kennedy.

**The Grull Queens.** There are hundreds of Grull queens, but four are noteworthy. Since their names are unpronounceable to non-Grull, they have chosen the names of stones so humans have a way to distinguish them:

- **Shale** — the most high-profile queen, the first to make contact with the Kralkin. Her brood devised the first catapults able to launch Grull into space at high speeds. If you meet a Grull anywhere but Grull Prime, she is probably from Shale's brood.
- **Granite** — before Shale's rise to fame, Granite was considered the high queen of Grull Prime. All Grull queens are generally amicable toward each other, but Granite appears to hold some contempt toward Shale. Most of the Grull on Grull Prime are from Granite's brood.
- **Pyrite** — "Queen of the Underworld." While most Grull dwellings are in caves, Pyrite's brood prefers to live much deeper underground in complex cave networks. Her brood is known to trade in rare stones and minerals.
- **Pumice** — a troublemaker among the queens, with no apparent ends to her actions beyond stirring the pot. She is the most mobile of the queens and is often directly involved in her schemes. Her brood is very small but has very distinct features, such as jet-black skin and unusually shaped horns.
