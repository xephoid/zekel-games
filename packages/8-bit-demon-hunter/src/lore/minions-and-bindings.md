---
title: Minions and bindings
summary: The demon's servants hidden among the townsfolk, how they lie, and the ten bindings they keep that weaken their master.
tags: demon, minions, bindings, houses
---

The demon does not work alone. In every town a few ordinary people, two or so, serve it in secret: its minions. They look like anyone. They have trades, pets, favourite colours, houses. The one thing that gives them away is that, about the demon, they always lie. Ask a minion about the demon and the rumour you get back is false, every time. The demon itself lies the same way.

Nobody knows how a person becomes a minion. The old stories say the demon offers a bargain, as it once did to Memlax. Finishing a minion's errand does not buy their gratitude: they laugh and tell you that you will never find their master.

**The bindings.** Each minion keeps a relic in their house, behind a locked door: a demon binding. There are ten names for them in the old lists: Angel's Lock of Hair, God's Breath, Eye of the Cyclops, Rune Engraved Marble, Orb of Flesh, The First Shuckle, King's Goblet, Basilisk Scale, Dragon Blood, Vampire's Fang. What each one is, nobody can say for certain; what they do is agreed: every binding a hunter carries weakens the demon, and with enough of them it can be killed with a single blow.

**The locks.** Every house in every town is locked, and the townsfolk do not open them for strangers. A Locksmith's favour opens one. A Lady Bandit's lockpick opens one, at a thief's price. An innocent's house holds nothing of note; a minion's holds a binding. Searching a house does not tell the town what you were looking for.

**The Tailor's eye and the Musician's ear.** Tailors claim they can tell whether anyone in their town is lying; Musicians claim to know how many minions their town hides. Neither will say who.
