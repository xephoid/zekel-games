---
title: The Demon
summary: What is known of the demon that hides among the townsfolk, what it wants, and the monsters that came with it.
tags: demon, monsters, minions, history
---

Nobody has seen the demon's true face. It hides as an ordinary person in one of the five towns, with a trade, a pet, a favourite colour and an item like anyone else, and it has minions in every town who lie for it. It is the same demon the old stories tell of: the one that took the wizard Memlax, ruled through him, and was pushed out, not killed, by his wife Chineera and her followers. Now it is pushing back.

**What it does.** Since it returned the land has filled with monsters; people say it summoned them. It grows stronger, the stories say, the more people forget the old tales, and it likes to appear as a very helpful stranger. Its minions keep bindings that weaken it; with enough of them it can be killed with one blow. Those who can sense it are said to be descendants of Chineera.

**Its lie.** Ask the demon about the demon and it tells you, sincerely, something false. That is its one tell, and its minions share it.

**The hunt.** A stranger has come to find it. Townsfolk trade rumours, favours and old stories; some hold real secrets, earned by doing them a service. Accuse the wrong person and the demon is gone for good, laughing. Accuse the right one and the town falls away into its arena: black pillars, a thing with too wide a grin, and three monsters it calls when it is hurt.

What the townsfolk say:

- The world didn't used to be filled with monsters. People say the demon summoned them here.
- All the monsters showed up about the same time as rumors of the demon did.
- The demon ruled this world a long time ago and has returned.
- The demon doesn't die. The sorceress didn't kill it; she just pushed it out. And now it's pushing back.
- Old stories say the demon doesn't always show up as a monster. Sometimes it shows up as a very helpful stranger.
- The demon is said to grow stronger the more people forget the old stories.
- The demon has minions everywhere. Its minions keep special bindings that weaken it; weaken it enough and it can be killed with one hit.
- Some say the demon can mimic people's abilities, making it harder to find.
- There's an old journal in the wall of a house: one word in it, in the old language, means "demon".
