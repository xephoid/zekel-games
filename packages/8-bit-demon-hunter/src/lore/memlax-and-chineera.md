---
title: Memlax and Chineera
summary: The wizard who made a deal with a demon to save his dying wife, the sorceress who beat it, and the song people still cry at.
tags: history, memlax, chineera, the deal, songs
---

**Memlax.** Long ago there was a wizard named Memlax, and the grandmothers say he was the kindest wizard who ever lived: he healed for nothing, settled quarrels, taught children their letters. His wife Chineera fell ill. Some say a demon poisoned her; some say she just got sick. Memlax made a deal with a demon to save her. What he gave up, nobody agrees on; everyone agrees it was something awful. Over the years he turned, and in the end he ruled the world with an iron fist. Some say the demon possessed him; some say it simply took him, a little at a time. The frightening part, people say, is that he was not always evil: it can happen to anyone.

**Chineera.** She lived, and she loved him even after the demon took him. That is the hard part of the story and the part the songs are about. With her followers, the rebellion the towns still half-remember, she fought the demon that wore her husband. She did not kill it; she pushed it out. The demon thought love was a weakness; she proved it was the opposite. Her descendants, people say, can sense the demon, and some of them are alive in the towns today.

**What is not known.** What the deal cost. Whether the illness was the demon's doing. Exactly how she pushed it out: love, the five artifacts, or a great many people dying. Where the demon went, and what let it back.

**The song.** There is an old song about a sorceress and a broken heart. Nobody remembers all of it. Mothers sing it to children and cry at the end.

What the townsfolk say:

- There is an old story about a powerful wizard that made a deal with a demon to save his dying wife.
- Long ago there was a wizard named Memlax; over time he turned evil and ruled the world with an iron fist.
- Some say the evil wizard Memlax was possessed by a demon.
- The old stories say Memlax wasn't always evil, which is more frightening than if he had been.
- Nobody asks what the wizard actually gave up to save his wife. Must have been something awful.
- The demon was defeated by Chineera and her followers once, a long time ago.
- My grandpa told me the demon was married to Chineera, but that doesn't make sense.
- Chineera, once the bride of Memlax. Those who can sense the demon are her descendants.
- My gran used to say Chineera won because the demon underestimated her. It thought love was a weakness. She proved it was the opposite.
- There's an old song about a sorceress and a broken heart. It always made my mother cry at the end.
- If someone offers you a deal that seems too good to be true, you walk away. That's how the whole world almost ended once.
