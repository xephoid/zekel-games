---
title: The clearings
summary: The named open places between the towns and temples, and what each is known for.
tags: places, clearings, map
---

Between the towns lie the clearings: open ground where the paths cross, where monsters gather and where plants, trees and ore are found. Each world has a handful of them, drawn from these:

- **Mossy Hollow** — a damp dip where everything grows green and slimes breed.
- **The Old Crossroads** — four paths and a leaning signpost nobody repaints.
- **Bramble Flats** — low thorn scrub; bandits like the cover.
- **Foggy Meadow** — mist until noon; travellers lose their bearings and their company.
- **Stonefield** — rocks everywhere, some with ore in them, the kind you blow open.
- **Windy Rise** — a bald hill with a view of three towns on a clear day.
- **The Burnt Glade** — black stumps from a fire nobody remembers starting.
- **Cricket Bend** — a bend in a dry stream, loud at night.
- **Heron Marsh** — reeds and standing water; snakes.
- **Lantern Heath** — named for lights seen there after dark; people blame wizards.
- **Thistle Downs** — rolling and prickly; sheep went missing here first.
- **Ruin Row** — a line of broken pedestals and old stone; the rebellion is blamed or credited, depending who you ask.

Plants and trees can be harvested by anyone with a blade and time, though hacking at them draws attention. Ore deposits need a bomb.
