Each world has five towns, drawn from eighteen that people know by name. Every town has ten people, one of every trade (Carpenter, Barber, Soldier, Musician, Farmer, Blacksmith, Merchant, Tailor, Locksmith, Mayor), an inn, and a row of locked houses. The towns are walled and the monsters do not come in.

**Money and trade.** The coin is the shuckle. Inns buy and sell plant fiber, wood, demon powder, demon ichor, iron ore and gold, each town at its own prices and with its own stock; a traveller with a cart and a good memory can make a living on the difference. An inn bed heals any wound for five shuckles. Inns also train fighters for a fee and run carts between towns.

**The trades and their favours.** Farmers can tell whether a stranger has already met the demon ("you have the look"). Musicians hear how many minions their town hides. Barbers know everyone and will introduce you. Tailors can tell if anyone in town is lying. Mayors keep a little gold. Merchants pay for errands in shuckles. Soldiers train you. Blacksmiths improve your gear. Carpenters know which towns the demon is not in. Locksmiths open doors.

**The towns, as people speak of them:**

- **Himfergen** — oldest of them, its doors all facing away from the eastern ruins.
- **Walgon** — a river town; the ferryman's family has run it for generations.
- **Sheeford** — sheep country, and nervous since the sheep started vanishing.
- **Malpance** — proud, a little grand, with the only paved square.
- **Placington** — "the place in the middle", a crossroads market.
- **Townville** — plain as its name and happy that way.
- **Cityberg** — calls itself a city; it is not.
- **Villageton** — orchards and a famous cider.
- **Littleburg** — small and close; everyone related to everyone.
- **Smalltown** — smaller still, and suspicious of Littleburg.
- **Bigton** — biggest inn in the region, and the loudest.
- **Clockford** — a clock tower that has not told the right time in living memory.
- **Someplaceville** — the butt of every joke, and the kindest people.
- **Anyton** — named by a mayor who could not be bothered.
- **Locationberg** — map-makers settled it; the signposts are excellent.
- **Farmford** — grain, cattle and the best Farmer in the land.
- **Bakersburg** — bread you can smell from the clearing.
- **Smithton** — forges day and night; the Blacksmith here is famous.

What the townsfolk say of daily life: prices differ town to town; heal at the inn, five shuckles is a bargain; someone says they bought a bomb off a Lady Bandit, though nobody can swear to it; rocks can be blown open for ore; watch out for the mushrooms; people here act as if the old days were just stories, but stories come from somewhere.
