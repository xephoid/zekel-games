---
title: The temples
summary: Five ancient temples stand in the wild places; townsfolk know only that they are very old and very dangerous, and that nobody who goes in comes back.
tags: temples, places, danger
---

Five temples stand in the wild places between the towns, each at the end of a path from a clearing: the Sky Temple, the Earth Temple, the Space Temple, the Light Temple and the Fire Temple. Those are names on signposts; nobody in the towns knows who built them or when. They are older than the towns, older than the rebellion, older than any story.

**What the townsfolk know.** They are dangerous. People who go in do not come out, or come out changed and will not talk about it. There is something inside them, the legends say: the five artifacts of the old stories, maybe, or treasure, or nothing but death. Children are told not to go near them. The Sky Temple is said to be walled in by a ring of stone with no gate. That is the whole of it. A townsperson asked what guards a temple, or what lies in it, has nothing but rumour and a shrug: "something terrible", "something powerful", "I wouldn't go".

**What the townsfolk do not know.** What creatures live inside. What the artifacts are called or what they do. That there is a riddle door or a gauntlet. Any of the names below.

---

**For the GM only. No townsperson knows any of this; use it to narrate once the player is inside.**

- *Sky Temple*: inside a ring of stone wall with no gate. Guarded by bees the size of dogs that come straight at you. Holds the Winged Boots.
- *Earth Temple*: low and dark, its hall choked with Man Eater Flowers that lie still until you are close. Holds the Aura of Protection.
- *Space Temple*: cold, its walls scratched with star maps nobody can read. Arachne nest in its corners. Holds the Cape of Teleportation.
- *Light Temple*: bright even at night. Floating Eyes drift its corridors and see you long before you see them. Holds the Eye of Truth.
- *Fire Temple*: warm to the touch from outside. Fire Skulls race its halls. Holds the Fire Bombs.

Each temple has two ways to its heart: a gauntlet where its guardians come one at a time, and a sealed door carved with a riddle. The guardians drop demon ichor. A gauntlet left half done is full again on return. The player learns all of this by going in; the engine's responses state it.
