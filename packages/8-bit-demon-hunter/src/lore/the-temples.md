---
title: The five temples
summary: Sky, Earth, Space, Light and Fire: what each temple is like, what guards it, and what it holds.
tags: temples, artifacts, guardians, riddles
---

Five temples stand in the wild places between the towns, each on a path from a clearing. Nobody goes in; everybody has a story about someone who did. Each temple has two ways to its heart: a gauntlet, where its guardians come at you one at a time, and a sealed door carved with a riddle that only opens to the right answer. People who know the old stories do better at the doors.

- **The Sky Temple** stands inside a ring of stone wall with no gate. Its guardians are bees the size of dogs that come straight at you. It holds the Winged Boots: with them on, you walk the air above the monsters, and only those that can shoot or throw can reach you. The Sky Temple's riddle is said to be about the artifacts themselves.
- **The Earth Temple** is low and dark, its hall choked with Man Eater Flowers that lie still until you are close. It holds the Aura of Protection, a stillness nothing can break while you keep it, though you can strike nothing through it either.
- **The Space Temple** is cold, its walls scratched with star maps nobody can read. Arachne nest in its corners. It holds the Cape of Teleportation, which carries its wearer to any town they have walked in.
- **The Light Temple** is bright even at night. Floating Eyes drift its corridors and see you long before you see them; their gaze burns from far away. It holds the Eye of Truth: look at a person and know whether they are lying.
- **The Fire Temple** is warm to touch from outside. Fire Skulls race its halls. It holds the Fire Bombs: a flame that never runs out.

Each temple's guardians drop demon ichor, the thick black stuff that inns pay well for. Those who leave a gauntlet half done find it full again when they return.
